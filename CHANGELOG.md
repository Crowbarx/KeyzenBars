# Keyzen Bars

## 1.0.2

- Pet bar: Blizzard's pet buttons become a movable Keyzen bar, shown while you have a pet.
- Search: type in the box above General to find any setting, with its controls right in the results.
- Menu & Bags is now Utility Bars, with tabs for the stance, pet, totem, menu and bag bars.

## 1.0.1

- Shamans: the call totem bar is now a movable Keyzen bar, with its options under Menu & Bags.
- The Background sliders are gone from the options window, since the public themes have no background art.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Keyzen keypad bar, Naga grid, stance bar and extra bars.
- Keybinds from the bar, micro menu and bag bars, window buttons.
- Experience bar with session, rate, kills and quest XP info.
- Profiles with module copy, themes and the /kz options window.
